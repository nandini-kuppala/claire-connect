The idea in one minute

Why this, why now

The problem
  • ~₹35,000 cr of domestic trade a year runs on WhatsApp photo floods and phone calls.
  • Makers speak Tamil; buyers speak Hindi, Bengali, Telugu. Traders in the middle handle ~75 % of volume.
  • One order passes through ~8 job-work units, tracked on paper challans.
  • Payments slip; there's no shared record of who pays on time.
What we build
  • A WhatsApp-style chat app where Claire turns speech into searches, quotes and orders.
  • Translated text and voice notes between any two businesses.
  • Matching that combines 2–3 sellers when no one has the full quantity.
  • Two-way ratings, a Trade Desk, and job-work splitting on the same rails.
Why it can win
  • Hits all three track ideas: 1-on-1 deal chat, agent dispatch, translated chat.
  • A real problem with real pilot partners (SS Garments, Barath Garments, Janshi Text).
  • Demo moment judges remember: Hindi voice in, Tamil voice out.
  • Real users onboarded this weekend count toward the traction bonus.

Journey 1 · Onboarding

Two minutes, one typed field

Lakshmi only types her phone number. Everything else she says out loud, and her profile fills in on screen while she talks.

  1. Phone + OTPOTP auto-reads on Android. Nexconn chat token is issued in the background.
  2. Tap your languageBig script tiles: தமிழ் · हिन्दी · English · తెలుగు … or just speak and we detect it.
  3. Talk to Claire (≤ 4 questions)Name and business · what you make, sell and buy · where you are · GST (optional, skip in one tap).
  4. Confirm the profile card"Yes, correct", or say what to fix.
  5. Sellers: share photosPick WhatsApp photos or use Share → CLAIRE. Claire groups them into products. Say "All 180, minimum 500, M to XXL" once.
  6. Land in the inboxClaire pinned on top. Sellers get a storefront link to share on WhatsApp; buyers see matching sellers.
FallbackSilent for 8 seconds, or Claire misunderstands twice? Claire offers "Shall someone from our team call you?" and a Trade Desk agent finishes onboarding on a call.

Buyers get an answer in minutes, even when the owner is on the floor

Quote ₹172Send catalogCan't doLet Claire handle

Or quote by voice: "172, four days, 30 percent advance" becomes an offer card.

Auto-reply modes
  • Off · nothing automatic.
  • Suggest (default) · Claire drafts, seller taps Send.
  • Auto · after 2 min of silence Claire answers from the seller's own catalog and stock, labelled "Auto-reply by Claire".

Never automatic: accepting an order, credit terms, or going below the floor price.

Keep data fresh by voice
  • "2,000 track pants ready" updates stock.
  • "Machines free for 3,000 pieces this week" updates capacity.
  • "All track pants +5 % from Monday" revises rates and notifies open quotes.
  • Morning digest: new enquiries, offers waiting, payments overdue.

Journey 4 · Order to payment

One order card that updates itself

The card lives at the bottom of the deal chat. Each change also posts a short notice in "CLAIRE Updates" in both languages.

1ConfirmedOffer accepted. Code CC-24-00123.
2Advance paidBuyer adds UPI/NEFT ref; seller confirms in one tap.
3In productionOptional voice updates: "600 done today".
4Dispatched"Sent by KPN, LR 55821, 12 bundles" + LR photo.
5DeliveredBuyer confirms or taps "Report a problem".
6Balance paidDue = delivery + credit days. Nudges at +3 and +7 days; ticket at +15.
7Rate each otherBoth sides rate; scores reveal together.
Credit ladder

First order: 50–100 % advance. After 3 clean orders Claire asks the seller: "Amit has paid on time 3 times. Offer 15 days credit?" Partners can go to 30 days. CLAIRE never lends money.

45-day alert

If the seller is a registered micro/small manufacturer or job worker, the buyer sees "Pay within 45 days to claim this as an expense" (Section 43B(h)) and the seller sees days outstanding.

Paperwork hints

GST at 5 % for garments up to ₹2,500 a piece. "E-way bill needed" for inter-state loads over ₹50,000. The LR + invoice photo is what buyers release payment against.

Journey 6 · Reorder and partners

Good trades turn into standing relationships

One-tap reorder

Orders → Reorder, or say "same as last time from Sree". The seller gets a reorder card: Accept at ₹168 or quote a new price.

Smart reminder

Amit reordered about every 30 days twice. On day 28 Claire asks: "Time to reorder 1,000 track pants from Sree Textiles?"

Partner

After 2 good orders, Claire suggests a partnership: pinned chat with a standing price card, priority in matching, optional credit days, early access to new stock.

The same matcher works on capacity instead of stock. Each accepted line becomes a work order that doubles as a digital delivery challan.

Job offers go out

Each unit gets a card: garment, quantity, rate, deadline, "material supplied", a photo of the style. Accept, counter or decline. A decline triggers a replacement suggestion.

Progress by voice

Lakshmi says "Today 600 done" and Ravi's progress bar moves to 600 / 2,000. Claire asks once a day if no update came in.

Return, pay, rate

"Returned 1,980, rejected 20" closes the challan. Payment due = pieces × rate. Both rate each other. Ravi (Tamil) and Raju (Hindi) never needed a common language.

The same flow covers raw material: Lakshmi says "Need 300 kg single jersey, 180 GSM, white, this week" and gets fabric sellers by the kg. Job workers with idle machines can post capacity and get pinged when someone searches.
Journey 8 · Trade Desk (human agent dispatch)

The right person, in the right language, inside the same chat

Nexconn has no built-in agent routing, so we build it from groups, presence, targeted messages and webhooks. That is itself a strong story for the judges.

What makes it stand out

Seven things judges should remember

  • Onboarding by conversation. A live profile fills in while you talk; a catalog from photos already on your phone.
  • Hindi voice in, Tamil voice out. Two people with no shared language close a ₹1.68 lakh order.
  • Combo orders. No one has 1,000? Two sellers with the same fabric do, together.
  • The whole chain. Yarn, fabric, job work and finished goods on the same rails, with digital challans.
  • Trust from records. Buyers earn "pays on time" from real payment dates; references replace brokers.
  • A dispatch center built on Nexconn. Language-skill routing, agent joins the same chat with a summary.
  • Seller auto-reply. Buyers hear back in minutes while the owner is on the factory floor.
