# Trade glossary (en / ta / hi)

This fixes one word per concept before UI strings and Claire's templates are written (03 §12).

**Status:** every Tamil and Hindi entry is ***to verify*** until a native speaker from the Tiruppur trade signs it off. Field-test the alternatives marked *alt.* before the flag goes on for real users.

**Rules:**
- Prefer the spoken trade word over the formal one.
- Keep "kg", "DC", "GSM", lot codes, sizes and business names in Latin script.
- Use Western digits with Indian grouping (1,20,000).
