# Job work under GST: rules, paperwork and product implications

**Research date:** 20 Nov 2026. **Scope:** Tiruppur (Tamil Nadu) knitwear SMEs. A principal sends yarn, fabric, panels or trims to a job worker for knitting, dyeing, printing, stitching and similar work. Ownership stays with the principal.

**Confidence tags:**
- **[verified]**: read in the primary text (Act, Rule, notification, circular, form, or portal advisory), or in a gazette copy hosted by a government or professional body.
- **[likely]**: consistent secondary sources, or primary text read only through a summary.
- **[uncertain]**: conflicting, stale or unconfirmed. Check with a CA before relying on it.

> This is product research, not tax advice. A CA should review it before any of it goes into user-facing text. The rules below cover the law as amended up to Notification 05/2026-Central Tax (26 Oct 2026) and 04/2026-Central Tax (26 Oct 2026).

## 0. Ten things the product team should know

1. **Job work is only "job work" if the owner is a registered person.** S.2(68) defines job work as a "treatment or process undertaken by a person on goods belonging to another registered person". An unregistered owner has no s.143 facility, no ITC-04 and no 5% job-work rate. [verified]
2. **The challan is the core document.** Goods go to the job worker without tax under a challan issued by the principal (Rule 45). The challan must carry the Rule 55 particulars: serial no. (≤16 chars), date, both parties' name, address and GSTIN, HSN, description, quantity, taxable value, place of supply (inter-state) and signature. [verified]
3. **Return deadlines.** Inputs must come back, or be supplied from the job worker's premises, within **1 year**. Capital goods have **3 years**. Moulds and dies, jigs and fixtures, and tools are exempt from both limits. If the deadline is missed, the dispatch is a **deemed supply** on the date the goods were originally sent out, with tax plus interest. The Commissioner can extend by up to 1 more year (inputs) or 2 more years (capital goods). [verified]
4. **ITC-04 is the only intimation.** It is filed **annually** (due 25 April) if the principal's previous-year aggregate turnover is ≤ ₹5 crore, and **half-yearly** (25 Oct and 25 Apr) if it is > ₹5 crore. This has applied since 1 Oct 2021 (Notification 35/2021-CT). It needs, per challan: job worker GSTIN (or *state* if unregistered), challan no./date, description, **UQC, quantity**, taxable value, input/capital-goods type, tax rates, nature of job work, and losses and wastes (UQC + quantity). [verified]
5. **E-way bill.** An **inter-state** job-work movement needs an e-way bill **regardless of value**. **Intra-state** movements need one above ₹50,000, unless the state exempts them. Tamil Nadu set its intra-state limit at **₹1 lakh** and, in 2018, exempted job work relating to yarn, fabric and garments (TN Notification 09/2018). I could not confirm that this TN exemption is still in force in 2026. [likely/uncertain]
6. **Rates since 22 Sep 2025.** Textile job work (Ch. 50–63) for a **registered** principal is **5%** (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST). Residual job work is 18%. Processing for an **unregistered** owner is 18%, except "tailoring services" at 5%. Yarn, fibre, fabric and knitted fabric are 5%. Garments are **5% up to ₹2,500 per piece and 18% above**. [verified]
7. **Unregistered job workers are allowed.** ITC-04 has an "unregistered" option, and the principal generates any e-way bill. To *sell directly* from an unregistered job worker's premises, the principal must declare those premises as an additional place of business. Waste and scrap at an unregistered job worker are supplied by the principal. No reverse charge applies on their job charges. [verified/likely]
8. **E-invoice threshold is still AATO > ₹5 crore** (Notification 10/2023-CT). It applies to the job worker's B2B job-charge invoices, not to challans. From 1 Jan 2027 it also covers RCM self-invoices, which do not affect normal job work (Notification 04/2026-CT). [verified/likely]
9. **2025–26 e-way bill platform changes:**
   - 2FA mandatory for everyone from 1 Apr 2025.
   - The base document (challan) must be ≤ 180 days old when the EWB is generated.
   - An EWB can be extended to at most 360 days after generation.
   - "Ship-To GSTIN" (or "URP") became mandatory in Bill-to/Ship-to cases from 1 Aug 2026, together with a new voluntary EWB "closure".

   The first three are [verified]. The Ship-To change is [likely].
10. **Recommendation in one line:** *record* everything in a compliance-ready structure. *Generate* only a printable delivery challan (DC) PDF on the user's own DC series, plus **draft** ITC-04 and e-way bill **exports** for the user or CA to review and upload. **Never** file on the GST or EWB portals ourselves, and never hold GST portal credentials. Section (c) has the details.

## (b) Delivery-challan field list for our "dispatch" record

The aim is to capture enough at dispatch and receipt time that a user can (1) print a Rule 55-compliant job-work challan, (2) fill the e-way bill Part A, and (3) produce ITC-04 Tables 4 and 5A/5B/5C later, **without re-keying**.

**Legend:**
- **Req (law)**: required by Rule 55, Rule 45, ITC-04 or EWB-01.
- **Req (us)**: needed for our own logic, such as deadline clocks or matching.
- **Opt**: nice to have.

### B.1 Dispatch header (one per challan)

| Field | Why / legal hook | Level |
|---|---|---|
| `dcNumber` | Rule 55(1): serial, **≤ 16 characters**, unique per GSTIN per FY. Prefix series are allowed, e.g. `JW/26-27/0042`. GSTR-1 Table 13 counts series ranges, so cancelled numbers must be kept, not reused. | Req (law) |
| `dcDate` | Rule 55(1)(i); ITC-04 col 3; EWB A.6. **Starts the 1-year/3-year clock** unless the goods went directly from a supplier. EWB generation is blocked if the DC is more than 180 days old. | Req (law) |
| `purpose` | `job_work_out`, `job_work_return`, `jw_to_jw`, `supply_from_jw_premises`, `own_use`, `other`. Maps to Rule 55(1)(b)/(c) and to EWB reason/sub-type (Outward 4 / Inward 6). | Req (law) |
| `principal` (owner business) | s.143: the principal is always the owner, even when a job worker issues or endorses the challan (JW → JW). | Req (us) |
| `consignor`: legal name, address, GSTIN or null, state + state code, PIN | Rule 55(1)(ii); EWB A.1/A.2 ("URP" if unregistered) | Req (law) |
| `consignee`: legal name, address, GSTIN/UIN or null, state + state code, PIN | Rule 55(1)(iii); ITC-04 col 1 (**GSTIN, or *state* if unregistered**); EWB A.3/A.4 | Req (law) |
| `consigneeRegType` | `regular`, `composition`, `unregistered`. Drives: ITC-04 job-worker type; who generates the EWB (principal if the job worker is unregistered); the additional-place-of-business warning for 5C; and the expected tax on job charges. | Req (us) |
| `placeOfSupply` (state) | Rule 55(1)(viii), required for inter-state movement. Derived from the consignee's state. | Req (law) if inter-state |
| `isInterState` (derived) | Triggers the **"EWB required regardless of value"** rule. | Req (us) |
| `natureOfProcess` | e.g. knitting, dyeing, compacting, printing, embroidery, cutting, stitching, checking/ironing/packing. ITC-04 "Nature of job work done" (5A/5B/5C col 9). Also used for SAC and rate hints. | Req (law, at return) |
| `goodsType` per line | `input` or `capital_goods`; plus flag `mouldsDiesJigsTools`. ITC-04 Table 4 col 9. Drives the 1-year / 3-year / no-limit clock. | Req (law) |
| `directFromSupplier` + `supplierInvoiceNo/Date` + `receivedByJWDate` | Circular 38 para 8.4(iv): the challan accompanies the supplier invoice. s.19(3)/(6) provisos: **the clock starts on the job worker's receipt date**. | Req (us) when applicable |
| `parentChallanRefs[]` | ITC-04 5A/5B/5C cols 7–8 (original challan no./date); JW → JW endorsements; piecemeal returns. Preserve the full chain. | Req (law) |
| `endorsement` (bool) + `endorsedBy` | Rule 45(1) provisos: a JW → JW move may be an endorsement of the principal's challan rather than a new DC. | Opt |
| Transport: `mode`, `vehicleNo`, `transporterName`, `transporterId` (GSTIN or TRANSIN), `lrNo`, `lrDate`, `approxDistanceKm` | EWB Part B / validity (1 day per 200 km). | Opt (Req if an EWB is generated) |
| E-way bill reference: `ewbNo`, `ewbDate`, `ewbValidUntil`, `ewbGeneratedBy` | Record only. Lets us warn before validity expires, and attach the EWB to the DC. | Opt |
| `signatoryName` + signature image | Rule 55(1)(ix): signature. | Req (law) for the printed DC |
| `copies` | Original / duplicate / triplicate labels on print. Rule 55(2) and Circular 38 (triplicate). | Req (us) for print |
| `expectedReturnDate` | Business turnaround, separate from the statutory deadline. | Req (us) |
| `statutoryDeadline` (derived) | dcDate (or JW receipt date) + 1 year for inputs, + 3 years for capital goods, none for moulds/dies/jigs/tools. Plus `extensionGrantedUntil` if the Commissioner extends. | Req (us) |
| `remarks`, photo of a paper DC | Practice: many DCs are still handwritten. Keep the image as evidence and link it to the structured record. | Opt |

### B.2 Dispatch lines (one per material, colour, lot or size line)

| Field | Why / legal hook | Level |
|---|---|---|
| `description` | Rule 55(1)(iv); ITC-04 col 4. Build it from material type and attributes, e.g. "Cotton yarn 30s combed, lot 2231", "Single jersey 160 GSM 30" dia, grey", "Cut panels: polo, navy, M". | Req (law) |
| `hsn` | Rule 55(1)(iv); EWB A.8. Store the **6-digit** HSN even if the user's turnover allows 4 digits, so we are never short. Templates: 5205 cotton yarn, 5509/5510 MMF staple yarn, 6006/6004 knitted fabric, 6109/6105/6110 knitted garments, Ch. 60 fabric pieces; cut panels are classified by the fabric's chapter [uncertain: check panel classification with a CA]. | Req (law) |
| `quantity` + `isProvisional` | Rule 55(1)(v) allows a provisional quantity. ITC-04 col 6. | Req (law) |
| `uqc` | ITC-04 col 5 and the losses-and-wastes UQC. Must be a **GST UQC code**, e.g. KGS, PCS, ROL, NOS, MTR, DOZ, SET, BAG, BOX, CTN, PAC, GMS. Our internal units must map to a UQC. | Req (law) |
| `secondaryQty` + unit | e.g. rolls + kg, bundles + pieces, cones + kg. Not legally required but how Tiruppur actually counts. | Opt |
| `taxableValue` | Rule 55(1)(vi); ITC-04 col 7 (Table 4); EWB A.7 (consignment value incl. tax, excl. exempt). For job work this is the **book/cost value** of the goods, not the job charge. | Req (law) |
| `taxRate` (CGST/SGST/IGST/cess %) | ITC-04 Table 4 cols 10–12 require the **rate** applicable to the goods. Rule 55(1)(vii) requires tax *amounts* only when the movement is a supply, so show rates but no tax amount on a job-work DC. | Req (law, rate) |
| `lot/batch`, `colour`, `size`, `style` | Our tracking model. Also makes the description specific enough to match returns. | Req (us) |

### B.3 Receipt (job work return) record, needed for ITC-04 Table 5

| Field | Why | Level |
|---|---|---|
| `receivedFrom` (JW) + `sameAsOriginalJW` (derived) | Decides **5A** (same job worker) vs **5B** (a different job worker, e.g. the dyer returns fabric the knitter received). | Req (law) |
| `jwChallanNo`, `jwChallanDate` | ITC-04 5A/5B cols 2–3. **Mandatory when the job worker issued a fresh challan**, which is always the case for piecemeal returns. | Req (law) |
| `receivedDate` | Stops the statutory clock for the quantity received. Sets the ITC-04 period. | Req (law) |
| `lines[]`: description, HSN, qty, UQC, `originalChallanRefs[]` | ITC-04 cols 4–8. Converted goods are often in a **different UQC** from what was sent (kg of fabric out, pieces back). Store both and do not force conversion. | Req (law) |
| `lossAndWaste`: qty + UQC | ITC-04 cols 10–11. Process loss in kg (dyeing shrinkage, cutting waste) and rejects. | Req (law) |
| `rejectsReturnedForRework` | Our model: a rework loop creates a new outward challan referencing the original. | Req (us) |
| `supplyFromJWPremises`: principal invoice no./date, buyer | ITC-04 **5C**. The invoice also goes in GSTR-1. | Req (law) when applicable |
| `jobChargeBill`: JW bill no./date, SAC (9988, e.g. 998821 textile, 998822 apparel [likely]), rate per kg/piece, amount, GST % (5 / 18 / 0 if unregistered / bill of supply if composition) | Not needed for ITC-04, but users want it (the "job work bill"). Also feeds cost and loss analytics. | Opt (v2) |

### B.4 Matching rule we must decide

ITC-04 Instruction 3 says the original challan columns "may not be filled where one-to-one correspondence ... is not possible". In practice, returns arrive in lots that mix several dispatches. **Product decision:** match returns to open dispatch lines **FIFO by default**, oldest challan first, so that statutory clocks close in the order they started. Let the user override the match, and show the allocation. Do **not** reset the clock when goods are re-dispatched after a return to the *same* chain without coming home [likely; see the terra-insight note in sources]. Note one exception: goods that come back to the principal and then go out again on a new challan *do* start a new clock, because the return closed the old one [likely].

## (c) Product implications: record vs generate

### C.1 What we should RECORD (all v1)

- Every challan out and in, with the B.1–B.3 fields. Required-by-law fields are flagged as "missing" rather than blocking: the user can save a draft, but sees a "not compliance-ready" badge.
- The challan chain (parent refs, endorsements, piecemeal returns), quantities in the original UQC, and loss and waste.
- Registration status and GSTIN of each counterparty, validated for format and checksum, and state code from the GSTIN.
- E-way bill number, date and validity, when one was generated elsewhere.
- A photo of the paper DC or EWB, attached to the record.

### C.2 What we COULD GENERATE, and the risk of each

| Output | Value to users | Legal/operational risk | Effort | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Printable DC PDF** (Rule 55 fields, triplicate copy labels, the user's own series, Tamil + English labels) | **High.** Replaces DC books, ends retyping, and the job worker gets a WhatsApp-able PDF. | **Low–medium.** A DC is a self-issued document with no portal submission, like the challans Tally/Zoho/Vyapar print. Risks: wrong HSN or value chosen by the user; duplicate or gap numbering; and **misuse to back-date or fabricate challans**. Mitigations: immutable serials; no edits after issue, only cancel-and-reissue with an audit trail; a "Generated by CLAIRE on <timestamp>" footer; the user confirms HSN, value and signatory. | Small | **Do it (v1 or v2).** |
| **ITC-04 working file** (Tables 4 / 5A / 5B / 5C for a chosen period, as Excel/CSV in the GST offline-tool column order, plus an exceptions list) | **High** for registered principals. ITC-04 is tedious, and its data is exactly our challan data. | **Medium.** Wrong period or turnover band; unmatched challans; UQC mistakes. Mitigation: label it "draft for review"; the user or CA imports it into the GSTN offline tool and files with their own login. We never file. | Medium | **Do it (v2), as an export, not filing.** |
| **E-way bill Part A JSON** (the portal's bulk-generation format: sub-type 4 / 6, doc type CHL) | Medium. Saves typing for inter-state job work. Most intra-Tiruppur movements are under ₹1 lakh or possibly exempt. | **Medium–high.** NIC changes the schema (e.g. Ship-To GSTIN and URP in Aug 2026), which creates maintenance cost. Generating the EWB itself needs portal or API credentials and a GSP/ASP arrangement. Holding a user's EWB login or 2FA would be a security and liability problem, and **we must not do it.** A wrong value or pincode means an invalid EWB at a checkpost (detention or penalty under s.129). | Medium, then ongoing | **Defer (v3, if users ask).** In v1, record the EWB number and **deep-link** to ewaybillgst.gov.in with a copy-ready field summary. |
| Filing ITC-04, EWB or e-invoice on the user's behalf (API) | High convenience | **High.** We would need to become or partner with a GSP; we would hold credentials and carry liability for filings. | Large | **Do not do.** Out of scope. |
| Job worker's tax invoice for job charges | Medium (for job workers) | Medium. It is a tax invoice: rate choice (5% vs 18%), e-invoice above ₹5 cr. | Medium | **Later (with the payments scope decision).** If done, generate it as a draft and leave e-invoicing to the user's GST software. |

### C.3 Recommendation: generate GST documents, or just record them?

**Record by default. Generate only what is self-issued, and only as a user-confirmed draft. Never file.**

1. **v1:** structured recording with a compliance-readiness indicator, plus a **printable DC PDF** on the user's own serial series. This directly replaces DC books and WhatsApp photos, with low legal risk.
2. **v2:** an **ITC-04 draft export** (Excel/CSV in offline-tool order, with exceptions). This is the strongest "why digitise" argument for registered principals.
3. **v3, only if demanded:** e-way bill bulk-JSON export. Until then, record the EWB number and deep-link.
4. **Never:** portal filing, storing GST/EWB credentials, or auto-choosing tax rates or HSN without user confirmation.

Every generated document carries a "prepared by the user; verify before use" line, and the DC series is immutable once issued.

### C.4 Other product rules that fall out of the law

- **Unregistered owner means no s.143 job work.** If the principal has no GSTIN, label the record "material sent for processing" (not "job work"), hide ITC-04, and hint that processing rates may be 18% rather than 5%. Do not lecture; just keep the data model the same.
- **Unregistered job worker.** Let the principal record it. ITC-04 shows the *state* instead of GSTIN. The EWB, if any, is generated by the principal on both legs. The "Supply from job worker premises" action warns that the premises must be declared as an additional place of business.
- **Different UQC in and out** (kg → pieces) is legal and normal. Show conversion only as an analytic, never as the recorded quantity.
- **Job worker's view.** The job worker holds goods "belonging to others", which matches the brief's ownership-vs-custody model. Job workers have no ITC-04 duty. They need the inward challan reference to issue return challans and their job-charge bills.

## (e) Practice in Tiruppur: delivery challans in reality

> Primary evidence is thin. Most of the points below are **[uncertain]** and should be validated in user interviews (Phase 1 checkpoint question).

- **Job work dominates the cluster.** Most units work on a job-work basis, dyeing is largely outsourced, and a garment passes through many separate units (TEA cited "at least 17 different processes" in 2018). [likely] ([BEE Tiruppur cluster report](https://beeindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/Tiruppur.pdf); [FashionNetwork 2018](https://in.fashionnetwork.com/news/Relief-in-Tiruppur-Tamil-Nadu-halts-intra-state-e-way-bill,964874.html))
- **Industry pushed for e-way bill relief on job work.** TEA lobbied for and welcomed the 2018 TN exemption for job work on yarn, fabric and garments. This suggests intra-Tiruppur challan movements mostly run without EWBs. [likely]
- **Paper DC books** (pre-printed, serially numbered, carbon triplicate) are the common format among SMEs. Many small job workers (home tailors, small stitching and ironing units) are **unregistered** and send goods back on the principal's copy or a handwritten slip. Photos of DCs go over WhatsApp as proof of dispatch and receipt. [uncertain: practitioner knowledge, not documented]
- **Fields typically printed on a Tiruppur job-work DC** (validate with real samples):
  - DC no. and date; from and to (name, address, GSTIN); vehicle no.
  - Description: yarn count, fabric type, GSM, dia, colour, lot/batch no., style and size for panels.
  - Rolls or bags or bundles, **weight in kg** (gross/net), pieces or dozens for panels and garments.
  - **Process** (e.g. "for dyeing: navy", "for compacting", "for stitching").
  - Return-of-goods note ("Not for sale – for job work only, to be returned"); receiver's signature; sometimes rate per kg or piece.
  - HSN and value are often missing on hand-filled books. [uncertain]
- **Compliance tightness.** Larger exporters (often > ₹5 cr turnover, ITC-04 half-yearly, e-invoicing) run ERP/Tally job-work modules. Small SMEs often reconstruct ITC-04 from DC books at year-end, or their CA files a minimal or nil return. [uncertain] The ~11–13% cumulative weight loss across hops (knitting, dyeing, cutting, stitching) makes year-end reconciliation hard if loss is not recorded per hop. [uncertain: single vendor source, [terra-insight, 6 Jul 2026](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/multi-hop-job-work-reconciliation-textile-india)]
- **"Job work bill".** The job worker bills job charges separately (per kg for knitting or dyeing, per piece for stitching). Registered job workers charge 5% GST (SAC 9988) to registered principals. Unregistered ones issue a plain bill. The bill is a *service* invoice, not the goods challan. [likely]
- **Tamil Nadu specifics.** TNGST mirrors CGST for Rules 45, 55 and 138. The TN-specific difference is the Rule 138(14)(d) notification (₹1 lakh intra-state limit and the 2018 job-work entry). No TN-specific job-work form exists. [likely]
