## Title
CLAIRE Connect: multilingual trade chat for India's knitwear supply chain | Nexconn Build Challenge 2026

## Description
CLAIRE Connect puts India's knitwear trade into one chat app, in each person's own language. It is built on the Nexconn Chat SDK.

Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, is India's knitwear capital: ₹46,000 crore of exports in FY26, and almost all of it is made by small businesses. Yet the trade still runs on trade fairs, phone calls and WhatsApp. Buyers travel or pay middlemen, makers sit on unsold stock, and one order passes through about eight businesses with no shared record.

What you'll see in the demo:
• Claire, an AI trade assistant. A buyer asks in Hindi and gets makers in Tiruppur, ranked by price, trust and delivery.
• A live deal on two phones. The buyer writes in Hindi, the maker reads it in Tamil, and her reply arrives in Hindi.
• A sample, then a private price tip from Claire. The deal is agreed at ₹94 and the order with GST appears on both phones.
• Ratings both ways. Buyers rate makers on quality, value and delivery; makers rate buyers on paying on time.
• Production tracking across the supply chain. Every hand-off is confirmed by both sides, and differences are flagged, never overwritten.
• The Flow view: where every piece of an order is right now.
• A private Claire that answers from your own records, in Tamil, Hindi or English.
• The Trade Desk: a human agent joins the same chat when something goes wrong.

Built on Nexconn:
• Chat SDK: direct, group and system channels
• Custom message cards (offers, orders, samples, hand-offs)
• Targeted messages for Claire's private tips
• Message translation in 10 Indian languages
• Message history, read receipts and typing indicators
• Webhooks
• Call SDK: voice and video calls
